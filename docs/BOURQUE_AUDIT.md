## Conclusion

The input chain was **sound** (identity, role, quote, probability), with three real findings: a false trade alert (stale source), a leg with no edge of its own carrying a ticket, and the same player on three tickets. The first and third are fixed; the second is documented. The losses themselves came from an 8-minute game, not from a mis-priced model. Three losses on one player's one bad night are **correlated, not three independent bad outcomes** — which is exactly why the per-player cap exists.
